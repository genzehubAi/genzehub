Download & install

About ten minutes
  1. Open MetaTrader 5, then File → Open Data Folder.
  2. Go into MQL5 then Experts, and drop GENZEFX.ex5 in there.
  3. Close and reopen MT5. GENZE appears in the Navigator panel under Expert Advisors.
  4. Open an XAUUSD chart, set it to M1, and drag GENZE onto the chart.
  5. On the Common tab, tick Allow Algo Trading. Paste your licence key into the Inputs tab.
  6. Load a preset that matches your broker, then press OK. The smiley in the top right means it is running.
  7. Check the Experts tab in the Toolbox. GENZE prints your break-even win rate there on startup — read it.

Stuck? Nobody gets penalised for asking. Message support and we will do it with you on a call.

Keep it running

VPS
The terminal has to stay online GENZE FX runs inside your MT5 terminal. If you close your laptop, it stops trading and any open positions are left unmanaged. Most people rent a small Windows VPS near their broker's server for a few dollars a month. Any VPS with 2 GB of RAM handles one MT5 terminal comfortably — what matters far more is low latency to your broker, not raw specs.
Trading carries risk of loss. Set your daily loss limit to a number you would genuinely be fine losing, and run it on a demo account for a couple of weeks before committing real money.
